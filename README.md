Campus Booking – INFR3120 Fall 2026 Group Project

Oue site lets Ontario Tech students book study rooms and networking and comp sci/engineering
lab equipment online. Students pick a lab and date, choose an open time slot, and the
booking appears in the public bookings table on the home page.
